Really interesting paper. Characterises the longitudinal problem with AI — such as skill erosion — and proposes a solution focused on the design of human-AI workflows that addresses all goals including human worthiness. Takes software development as a specific example but stays very abstract. The open question remains: how to apply these high-level principles to actually transform the SDLC.
